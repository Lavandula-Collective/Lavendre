**Lavendre** is a cozy, cottagecore-focused Minecraft modpack centered around exploration, farming, decoration, and custom item textures.

> 🪻 **A little more cozy. A little more you.**

Lavendre features a carefully curated collection of **CITs and resource packs**, alongside quality-of-life improvements, performance enhancements, and gameplay additions designed to complement the vanilla Minecraft experience.

### ✦ What's Included

* 🌸 Extensive **CIT collection** for custom items and decorations
* 🌿 Cottagecore-inspired **resource packs and visuals**
* 🧺 Farming, cooking, and cozy gameplay features
* ⚙️ Quality-of-life improvements
* 🚀 Performance-focused configuration
* 🏡 Carefully curated vanilla-friendly gameplay

### 📦 Staying Updated

Lavendre is actively developed, so new versions may include configuration changes, bug fixes, compatibility improvements, and new content.

**Keep your modpack updated to ensure you have the latest fixes and features.**
