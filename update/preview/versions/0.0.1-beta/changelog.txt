## Lavendre v0.0.1-beta

Welcome to the **first public beta** of Lavendre!

Built upon **Mizuno's 16 Craft**, Lavendre is a cozy Fabric modpack that modernizes the classic OptiFine CIT experience for modern Minecraft. Featuring curated CIT packs, beautiful visuals, and carefully selected quality-of-life enhancements, it's designed to make every world feel warm, immersive, and full of life.

### What's Included

- Modern Minecraft 1.21.1 support
- Mizuno's 16 Craft with community add-ons
- Thousands of curated CIT models
- Beautiful visuals and immersive enhancements
- Optimized Fabric performance
- Builder-friendly tools and quality-of-life features

### Before You Play

Some required resource packs cannot be included with the modpack due to their respective licenses.

After launching Lavendre, simply follow the in-game prompts to download the required packs and apply the recommended resource pack order.

### Known Issues

- This is a **beta** release. Bugs and incomplete features may still be present.
- Some third-party resource packs must be downloaded separately.
- Minor balancing, visual, and compatibility issues may still exist.
