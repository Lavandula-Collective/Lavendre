## Lavendre v0.5.1-beta

### Warning: Game-Breaking Update
This update makes major changes to modsn. A fresh install is highly recommended. You can back up your world and try it, but it may or may not work, and existing worlds could break.

A smaller update led by Vanilla Backport: Age of Mayhem, plus a round of recipe viewer fixes and mod updates.

## Highlights

### Vanilla Backport: Age of Mayhem

- Vanilla Backport is updated to Age of Mayhem, the big feature of this release.
- The creative inventory has been refreshed so the backported items sit in the right place.

### Copper Age Backport Removed

- Copper Age Backport and its Durability Fix are removed from the pack.

### Recipe Viewer Fixes

- Thatch blocks no longer show up grouped with helmets and hats.
- Handcrafted cushions and Minecraft cushions are now grouped separately.

### Behind the Scenes

- Default option configs now live in an external config, which makes future config updates smoother.
